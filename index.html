The Core Insight

Some experiences are best at the right moment.

In life, timing is everything. Some adventures have a perfect window — and when you know what that window is, you can plan to be there. That's the power of thinking in one-way and two-way doors.

🚪

One-Way Doors

These shine brightest at a specific life stage. Prioritize them.

  • High-altitude treks and physically demanding adventures
  • Family trips while kids are young enough to share it
  • Multi-generational travel while parents are healthy
  • Sabbaticals and career breaks before the cost compounds
  • Backpacking and long-duration travel before life anchors you
  • Diving, surfing, and endurance experiences
🔄

Two-Way Doors

Wonderful at any age — schedule these around your priorities.

  • Beach vacations and resort stays
  • City trips and cultural tourism
  • Food tours, wine country, culinary travel
  • Cruises (often better in your 60s anyway)
  • Domestic road trips and weekend escapes
  • Museum tours, historic sites
